United States · Data practices

Privacy Policy

What this static website stores, what the operator does not collect, and what outside providers may process.

Scope

This policy applies to Vlad Score and is written for its United States audience. It describes the website’s current operation. The operator does not run user accounts, payment processing, advertising technology or an audience database through this site.

Information handled by the site

The site does not request a name, postal address, payment information or active email submission. The release-notification field is currently inactive: entering or submitting an address does not transmit or store it.

The audio player saves two functional values in the browser’s local storage on the visitor’s device:

  • the last playback position;
  • the selected playback volume.

These values are not sent to the operator and can be removed by clearing the browser’s site data.

No sale, sharing or targeted advertising

The operator does not sell personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or use it for targeted advertising. The site has no advertising pixels or operator-controlled analytics. Because no sale or sharing occurs, a “Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information” mechanism is not presently required for the site’s own practices. Global Privacy Control signals are consistent with the site’s default behavior.

Hosting and technical requests

The site is delivered by GitHub Pages. GitHub states that a visitor’s IP address is logged and stored for security purposes. GitHub independently controls this hosting-side processing under its Privacy Statement.

Fonts and external links

The design requests typefaces from Google Fonts, so the visitor’s browser connects to Google to retrieve those files. Links to Amazon, GitHub documentation and other destinations lead to services with independent privacy practices. The operator does not control those third parties.

Cookies

The site’s own code does not set cookies. Hosting or external providers may use technical measures under their own policies. The site does not deploy advertising or analytics cookies and therefore does not display a consent banner for those technologies.

Children’s privacy

The site is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The inactive notification field should not be used to provide personal information.

Policy changes

If data practices change—for example, if release notifications, analytics or sales features become active—this policy will be revised before or when the new processing begins.
