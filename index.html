The music of two wars

The first is fought against Sultan Mehmed II. The second is fought against the image that will outlive Vlad. The score moves between both fronts: martial percussion, liturgical voices, dark-wave synths, strings, and the mechanical pulse of the printing press.

The novel behind the music

A prince fights for his country. A scribe builds his monster.

Vlad III writes against death. Matthes, a Saxon scribe, writes for survival. Between them lies the making of Dracula: altered words, inflated numbers, false letters, printed pamphlets, and a face cut into wood.

“I write because ink travels farther than a rider.”

The military war

Vlad against Mehmed II: tribute, hostages, winter raids, scorched earth, poisoned wells, the night attack, and the forest raised before an imperial army.

II

The narrative war

Vlad against the account made of him: Saxon reprisals, Hungarian necessity, Roman expectations, German printers, and horror made profitable.
